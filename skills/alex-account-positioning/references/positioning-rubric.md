# Positioning Rubric

Use this checklist:

## 1. Audience Specificity

- Is the audience concrete enough?
- Can the reader recognize themselves quickly?
- Is it narrower than "everyone interested in AI"?

## 2. Pain and Desire

- What recurring pain does the audience experience?
- What outcome are they chasing?
- Is the account speaking to both pain and aspiration?

## 3. One-Line Promise

- Can the account promise be said in one sentence?
- Does it sound different from generic AI content?
- Does it connect AI to a concrete reader outcome?

## 4. Content Pillars

- Are there 3-5 recurring pillars?
- Can these pillars support long-term publishing?
- Do they help reject irrelevant topics?

## 5. Differentiation

- Why should the reader trust this account?
- What angle, tone, or proof style sets it apart?
- Is it strategy-first, workflow-first, case-first, or operator-first?

## 6. Conversion Path

- What should happen after trust is built?
- Is the path realistic for the creator's current stage?
