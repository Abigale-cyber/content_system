# Title Rubric

Evaluate title samples across these dimensions:

## 1. Pattern

Classify whether the title is primarily:

- label
- contrast
- question
- suspense
- scene
- experience
- emotion
- emphasis

## 2. Driver

Look for dominant drivers such as:

- relevance
- utility
- curiosity
- emotion
- voice_of_reader

## 3. Hook Family

Useful operational families:

- question
- contrast
- numbered
- emotion
- identity
- direct

## 4. Reader Value

Judge whether the title sells:

- practical value
- emotional value
- identity expression
- cognitive upgrade

## 5. Alex Layer

Always end with:

- strongest reusable patterns
- patterns Alex should adapt instead of copy
- stale or generic patterns to avoid
